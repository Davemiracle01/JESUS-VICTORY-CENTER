Who We Are

Our Children's Ministry is committed to raising the next generation of bold, faith-filled disciples of Jesus Christ. We create a safe, fun and spiritually rich environment where every child can encounter God personally and build a strong foundation for life.

What We Do

📖

Sunday School

Age-appropriate Bible teaching every Sunday morning.

🎨

Creative Arts

Drawing, crafts and storytelling to bring Scripture to life.

🎵

Kids Praise

Fun, energetic worship songs that build love for God.

🙏

Prayer Training

Teaching children how to pray with faith and confidence.
