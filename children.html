Who We Are

Our Children's Ministry is committed to raising the next generation of bold, faith-filled disciples of Jesus Christ. We create a safe, fun and spiritually rich environment where every child can encounter God in a personal way and build a strong foundation for life.

Train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old he will not depart from it. Proverbs 22:6

What We Do

📖

Sunday School

Age-appropriate Bible teaching every Sunday morning.

🎨

Creative Arts

Drawing, crafts and storytelling to bring Scripture to life.

🎵

Kids Praise

Fun, energetic worship songs that build love for God.

🙏

Prayer Training

Teaching children how to pray with faith and confidence.

Join Us

We'd love to have you be a part of this ministry. Whether you're new to faith or have been walking with God for years — there's a place for you here.
