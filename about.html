Our Mission

Jesus Victory Center is a Christ-centered community dedicated to preaching the Gospel, strengthening faith, and transforming lives through love and truth. We welcome everyone and believe in spiritual growth through worship, prayer, and service to our community.

Our doors — and hearts — are always open. Come exactly as you are.

"Empowering God's people in all aspects of life thus enabling them in fulfilling God's purpose for their lives." — Acts 13:36

Our Vision

To ensure wholistic transformation of God's people in preparing them to positively impact and change their world for the glory of God.

"Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind." — Romans 12:2

Our Objectives

🌍 Propagate the Gospel

Seek and win the lost souls to Christ through evangelical preaching, evangelistic missions and crusades derived from the rallying call of Mark 16:15.

📖 Prepare & Equip Members

Preaching, teachings, Bible study groups, new believers classes, discipleship seminars and conferences.

🙌 Authentic Worship

Leading the body of Christ in spirit-filled, authentic praise and worship that ushers in the presence of God.

🏘️ Community Outreach

Serving Nairobi and surrounding communities through outreach, witnessing and practical acts of love.

Our Senior Pastors

Reverend Colonel Peter Kariukiwith Pastor Catherine Kariuki

Reverend Colonel Peter Kariuki and Pastor Catherine Kariuki are dedicated Gospel ministers and Christian leaders with over thirteen years of faithful Kingdom service. With a strong background in leadership and discipline from a distinguished military career, Rev. Col. Peter Kariuki brings wisdom, structure, and spiritual depth to ministry.

Together with his dear wife, Pastor Catherine Kariuki, they led the planting of Jesus Victory Center – Nairobi, a branch church of Ebenezer Global Church Ministries. Rev. Peter serves as the Senior Pastor, with strong support from Pastor Catherine, who is well versed in counseling ministry and pastoral care.

As a couple, they bring a dynamic blend of spiritual insight and guidance, driven by a deep desire to see people grow spiritually, fulfill their God-given destinies, and live passionately for Jesus Christ. They have been married for over twenty years and are blessed with a daughter, Grace Mukami, and a son, Alpha Baraka.
