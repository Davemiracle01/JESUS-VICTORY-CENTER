Ebenezer Global Church Ministries is a Spirit-filled, Word-grounded movement raising disciples and planting churches across Kenya and beyond. Their name — "Thus far the Lord has helped us" (1 Samuel 7:12) — echoes the faithfulness of God in every chapter of their story.
