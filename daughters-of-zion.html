Who We Are

Daughters of Zion is a Spirit-filled women's ministry dedicated to building women who are rooted in the Word, strong in their identity in Christ, and equipped to impact their families and communities. We celebrate womanhood as God designed it — beautiful, powerful and purposeful.

She is clothed with strength and dignity; she can laugh at the days to come. Proverbs 31:25

What We Do

📖

Bible Study

Deep study of the Word tailored for women of faith.

💆

Counselling

Pastoral care and support for women walking through challenges.

🌸

Empowerment Forums

Equipping women with life skills and spiritual tools.

🤝

Outreach

Community service and evangelism to women in need.
